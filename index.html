How to read this dashboard
  • BUY / SELL / WAIT – the model scores each market from -100 to +100. Beyond ±40 it gives a signal; otherwise it waits.
  • Entry, Stop, TP1–TP3 – where to get in, where the idea is wrong (stop), and three profit targets. “R” means multiples of your risk: 2R = win twice what you risk.
  • Lot size – calculated from your balance and risk % (change both in Settings).
  • Short horizon – signals use 1-minute prices, so levels are tight and short-lived.

Educational tool, not financial advice. Trading carries a high risk of loss.
